Privacy Policy

Acorn Ascent — last updated October 2026

Acorn Ascent does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. All game progress — your checkpoint, high score, acorn count, and squirrel name — is saved locally on your own device and is never sent to us or anyone else.

In-app purchases

The game offers one optional in-app purchase (the Accessory Pack), processed entirely through Apple's App Store payment system. We do not see or store your payment information — Apple handles that directly under its own privacy policy.

No tracking, no ads

Acorn Ascent contains no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party tracking of any kind. The app does not require an internet connection to play.

Contact
