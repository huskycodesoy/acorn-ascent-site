Frequently asked

I lost all my hearts and got sent back — is that a bug?
No — running out of all three hearts drops you back to the start of your current world. Every third acorn you eat refills a heart, so keep eating along the way to stay protected.

Do I need an internet connection to play?
No. Acorn Ascent is fully playable offline. An internet connection is only used to process the optional Accessory Pack purchase through Apple's App Store.

Will my progress sync between devices?
Not currently — progress is saved locally on your device only.
